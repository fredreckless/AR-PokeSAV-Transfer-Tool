Fast save: 5 runs, all CRC 59F4A131 (save #33); ~50 s (about 24 s per 64 KB bank -

Cartridge report: DAFFECEC, unchanged.
New finding: twice, the first run after a start-up returned the first 3-5 bytes of its output

=== Session 9, 25 Oct 2026: cold start, speed model, soak ===
Cold start: hello -> server mode; first save after it read cleanly (no garbling; guard silent).
Soak: 21 saves in a row, all CRC 59F4A131, 47.4-47.9 s each; no re-reads, re-sends or errors.
Speed: time = ~2.6 s fixed + ~18 ms per 8-byte packet actually sent (one GBA packet + one PC
  reply, ~9.2 ms each) - confirmed from 1% to 100% compressible data (FF padding 64 KB: 5 s;
  ROM code 64 KB: 151 s; this save's bank: 24 s, RLE 14%).
Ceiling: zlib packs this save's banks to ~3.9 KB vs RLE's ~9 KB; a GBA-side LZ compressor
  could bring a full save to roughly 25 s. Packet timing itself is fixed by the USB link.
